🎯 Pitch

Our app aims to help sexual assault victims. One of our apps most important features is the recording feature which triggers once the user says their safeword(a trigger word placed by the user). This feature is created to help victims gather evidence of the assault as it happens, in hopes of picking up anything that could prove the victims case in court, should they decide to press charges.

🧠 Why We Built This

Every 2 in 3 women are sexually assaulted in the united states each year. Yet 4 in 5 reported perpatrators are not charged with a crime. This shows how difficult it is for sexual assault victims to prove theri case in court. The justice system is very tedious it requires prosecution a lot of time and money to fight their case. We made this app as a means to make fighting your case in court easier. That hopefully in this audio recording the perpatrator may say something revealing their identity or affirming the lack of consent. Furthermore with our app we seek to make a space where women(cis or trans) could have a space to receive the care they needed to heal and process their own experiances with sexual assault.
