"Your voice matters"

Pitch

Have you ever been made to feel uncomfrotable on a bus or train? Maybe somebody was looking at you in a way that made you feel unsafe. Or they said or did something to you inappropriate. The scary thing is these incidents could happen anywhere even in the comfort of your own home. But while dealing with the emotional, mental, and physical devastation of sexual assault, victims are also left to fight a legal battle during such a vulnerable moment. having to prove to those around you that what happend to you actually happened. Speak up is an app meant to help make this legal process a little easier for victims as well as creating an environment where they can learn to process and hopefully heal from such traumatic events.

Why We Built This

Every 2 in 3 women are sexually assaulted in the united states each year. Yet 4 in 5 reported perpatrators are not charged with a crime. This shows how difficult it is for sexual assault victims to prove theri case in court. The justice system is very tedious it requires prosecution a lot of time and money to fight their case. We made this app as a means to make fighting your case in court easier. That hopefully in this audio recording the perpatrator may say something revealing their identity or affirming the lack of consent. Furthermore with our app we seek to make a space where women(cis or trans) could have a space to receive the care they needed to heal and process their own experiances with sexual assault.
